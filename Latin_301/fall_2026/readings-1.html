Latin 301
Readings 1

1
Iohannes Dei gratia rex Angliae, Dominus Hiberniae, dux Normanniae, Aquitanniae et comes Andegaviae, archiepiscopis, episcopis, abbatibus, comitibus, baronibus, iustitiariis, forestariis, vicecomitibus, praepositis, ministris et omnibus ballivis et fidelibus suis salutem.
  • Iohannes: MAS SING NOM = "John"
  • Normanniae: FEM SING GEN = "Normandy"
  • Aquitanniae: FEM SING GEN = "Aquitaine"
  • comes: = def. 4 = "Count"
  • Andegaviae: FEM SING GEN = "Anjou"
  • comitibus: = def 2 = "earl"
  • baronibus: MAS PLUR DAT = "baron"
  • iustitiariis: MAS PLUR DAT = "justiciar," a royal judge
  • forestariis: MAS PLUR DAT = "forester," an official in charge of a royal forest
  • praepositis: = "reeve," an overseer or administrator
  • ministris: = "officials"
  • fidelibus: = "feudal subjects"
  • salutem: = "greetings"; there is no main verb expressed in this sentence; it is implied by the presence of salutem, = "salutem dicit."
2
Sciatis nos intuitu Dei et pro salute animae nostrae et omnium antecessorum et heredum nostrorum ad honorem Dei et exaltationem sanctae Ecclesiae, et emendationem regni nostri, In primis concessisse Deo et hac praesenti charta nostra confirmasse, pro nobis et heredibus nostris in perpetuum quod Anglicana ecclesia libera sit, et habeat iura sua integra, et libertates suas illaesas;
  • Sciatis: present subjunctive → simple present volitive; an expression of will. Verb frame → SBJ knows X, where X is an ACC + INFIN indirect statement clause
  • nos: SBJ of concessisse ... et ... confirmasse
  • intuitu: MAS SING ABL = "consideration/respect"
  • exaltationem: = "glorification"
  • ad: prep + ACC(X) = "for/for the purpose of X"
  • in primis: = "especially"
  • concessisse et confirmasse: verb frame → SBJ grants and affirms X, where X is a quod + SUBJUNCTIVE clause. In such clauses quod may be translated "that"
  • in perpetuum: = "forever"
  • habeat: verb frame → SBJ keeps/maintains X in state Y, where X is an ACC noun and Y is ACC adj. agreeing with X in number and gender.
3
et ita volumus observari;
  • observari: there is no obvious subject for this verb. The implied SBJ is something like "the preservation of the laws and customs of the Church."
4
quod apparet ex eo quod libertatem electionum, quae maxima et magis necessaria reputatur Ecclesiae Anglicanae, mera et spontanea voluntate, ante discordiam inter nos et barones nostros motam, concessimus et charta nostra confirmavimus, et eam obtinuimus a domino papa Innocentio tertio confirmari;
  • quod: English often prefers to treat a Latin relative adjective as a demonstrative pronoun: quod = "this"
  • ex eo quod: = "from the fact that"
  • quod ... concessimus: a "that" clause.
  • magis: adv = "most."
  • reputatur: verb frame → SBJ(X) is considered PNOM(Y), where Y agrees with X in gender, number and case.
  • Ecclesiae: DAT(X) further specifies an adj: "to/for X"
  • motam: = "stirred up"
  • eam: SBJ of confirmari
  • obtinuimus: verb frame → SBJ arranges X, where X is a complementary infinitive clause.
5
quam et nos observabimus et ab heredibus nostris in perpetuum bona fide volumus observari.
  • quam: English often prefers to use a demonstrative where Latin has a relative: = "this"
  • et ... et: = "both ... and"
  • observabimus: = "preserve/maintain/respect"
  • ab: prep. + ABL(X) = "by X" in the sense of agency--"an action was carried out by X"
6
Concessimus etiam omnibus liberis hominibus regni nostri, pro nobis et heredibus nostris in perpetuum, omnes libertates subscriptas, habendas et tenendas eis et heredibus suis, de nobis et heredibus nostris.
  • Concessimus: verb frame → SBJ concedes X to Y, where X is ACC and Y is DAT.
  • habendas et tenendas: = "to be had and held"; think of the familiar line in wedding vows: "to have and to hold."
  • eis: effectively in apposition to hominibus; it recapitulates the early word and permits an expansion: "to them and their heirs."
  • de nobis: = "of us"; feudal rights are often said to be held by the lower "of" the higher.
7
Nullus ballivus ponat aliquem ad legem simplici loquela sua, sine testibus fidelibus ad hoc inductis.
  • ponat: present subjunctive → simple present volitive.
  • de cetero: adverbial = "henceforth"
  • ad legem: ponere ad legem = "to force to come to trial."
  • fidelibus: indicates social status as well as moral character: a fidelis is "oath-worthy" since such a person is included in feudal society because of oaths taken.
8
Nullus liber homo capiatur, vel imprisonetur, aut disseisiatur, aut utlagetur, aut exuletur, aut aliquo modo destruatur, nec super eum ibimus, nec super eum mittemus, nisi per legale iudicium parium suorum vel per legem terrae.
  • capiatur: present subjunctive → simple present volitive. The same applies to the coordinated present subjunctives.
  • disseisiatur: = "disseize" = "dispossess"
  • super: prep. + ACC(X) = "against X" as in an attack.
9
Nulli vendemus, nulli negabimus, aut differemus rectum aut iustitiam.
  • rectum: substantized, = "the right"
  • differemus: = "postpone."
10
Omnes mercatores habeant salvum et securum exire de Anglia, et venire in Angliam, et morari, et ire per Angliam, tam per terram quam per aquam, ad emendum et vendendum, sine omnibus malis toltis, per antiquas et rectas consuetudines, praeterquam in tempore guerrae, et si sint de terra contra nos guerrina;
  • habeant: present subjunctive → simple present volitive
  • salvum et securum exire: the infinitive is here functioning as a substantive, = "a safe and secure going out." This construction is not used in Classical Latin. venire and morari and ire are used in the same way as exire.
  • tam ... quam: = "so ... as." We can translate as "both ... and."
  • ad: prep. + ACC(X) = "for the purpose of X."
  • emendum: gerund, NEU SING ACC = "buying."
  • vendendum: gerund, NEU SING ACC = "selling."
  • toltis: = tolta, FEM PLUR ABL = "toll" (as in toll bridge). A mala tolta is an excessive tax imposed on the movement of persons or property through an area.
  • de: prep. + ABL(X) = "from X"
  • sint: present subjunctive → simple present potential
11
et si tales inveniantur in terra nostra in principio guerrae, attachientur sine damno corporum et rerum, donec sciatur a nobis vel capitali iustitiario nostro quomodo mercatores terrae nostrae tractentur, qui tunc invenientur in terra contra nos guerrina;
  • inveniantur: present subjunctive → simple present potential
  • attachientur: = "detain"; here a kind of protective custody
  • donec: subord. conj. = "until"
12
et si nostri salvi sint ibi, alii salvi sint in terra nostra.
  • nostri: substantive for nostri mercatores
13
Liceat unicuique de cetero exire de regno nostro, et redire, salvo et secure, per terram et per aquam, salva fide nostra, nisi tempore guerrae per aliquod breve tempus, propter communem utilitatem regni, exceptis imprisonatis et utlagatis secundum legem regni, et gente de terra contra nos guerrina, et mercatoribus, de quibus fiat sicut praedictum est.
  • Liceat: verb frame → SBJ is permitted to X, where SBJ is an infinitive clause and X is DAT. present subjunctive → simple present volitive
  • de cetero: = "henceforth/from now on"
  • salva fide nostra: leaving the kingdom does not dissolve the oaths between the king and his subjects. An ABL ABS clause: fide is SBJ and salva is PNOM (predicate nominal)
  • exceptis imprisonatis et utlagatis: another ABL ABS: exceptis is the verb of the clause and imprisonatis and utlagatis are substantivized SBJs.
  • fiat: present subjunctive → simple present volitive.
  • sicut: subord. conj. = "just as"
14
Quare volumus et firmiter praecipimus quod Anglicana ecclesia libera sit et quod homines in regno nostro habeant et teneant omnes praefatas libertates, iura, et concessiones, bene et in pace, libere et quiete, plene et integre, sibi et heredibus suis, de nobis et heredibus nostris, in omnibus rebus et locis, in perpetuum, sicut praedictum est.
  • praecipimus: = "command"; verb frame → SBJ comandes X, where X is a quod + subjunctive clause.
  • quod: subord. conj. = "that"
  • habeant et teneant: = "have and hold/ have and keep"
  • sibi: DAT(X) = "to/for X"; in this use it expresses the persons who benefit from the action of the verb of the clause
  • de: prep. + ABL(X) = "of/from X"; in the feudal idiom, a grantee holds rights "of/from" the grantor.
  • sicut: subord. conj. = "just as"
15
Iuratum est autem tam ex parte nostra quam ex parte baronum, quod haec omnia supradicta bona fide et sine malo ingenio observabuntur.
  • Iuratum est: verb frame → it was sworn that X, where X is a quod + indicative clause
  • tam ... quam: = "both ... and"
  • malo ingenio: = "evil intent"
16
Testibus supradictis et multis aliis.
  • Testibus ... aliis: ABL(X): = "with X"
17
Data per manum nostram in prato quod vocatur Runnymede, inter Windlesoram et Stanes, quinto decimo die Iunii, anno regni nostri decimo septimo.
  • Data: = "promulgated/published"
  • per: prep. + ACC(X) = "by X"
  • vocatur: verb frame → SBJ is called X, where X is NOM
  • Runnymede: NOM
  • Windlesoram: ACC, = "Windsor," a castle near London
  • Stanes: ACC, = "Staines," a town near London

William Wallace, hero of the Scots

18
Eodem anno inclitus ille athleta, Willelmus Wallace, Anglorum terror, filius nobilis militis eiusdem nominis, in Scotiam surrexit.
  • anno: ABL(X) = "at/in/on X"
  • athleta: = "champion"
  • surrexit: ≈ "came to prominence"
19
Erat enim statura grandis, corpore gigas, vultu decorus, visu iucundus et hilaris, amplis umeris, cruribus longis, ac toto corpore congruus et aspectu pulcherrimus.
  • statura ... corpore ... vultu ... visu: ABL(X) = "at/in/on X"
  • iucundus et hilaris: the idea seems to be that merely looking at WW cheered people up.
  • umeris: ABL(X) = "with X"
  • cruribus: ABL(X) = "with X"
  • corpore ... aspectu:= "balanced/well-proportioned"
  • aspectu: ABL(X) = "at/in/on X"
20
Vir mirandae fortitudinis, animi audacissimi, ac prudentiae singularis: omnibus amicis favorabilis ac inimicis terribilis apparens, in donis liberalis, in iudiciis aequissimus.
  • There is no main verb expressed in this sentence. The verb fuit should be understood.
  • Vir: PNOM; the SBJ of the (unexpressed) main verb us understood anaphorically from the preceding sentences.
  • mirandae: = "wonderful"
  • apparens: present participle → progressive active; verb frame → SBJ appears to be X, where X is adj agreeing with SBJ in GEN NUMB CASE.
  • aequissimus: = "most/very just/fair"
21
Qui cum verus esset Scotus, Anglorum nationem et eorum mores detestans, in principio suae rebellionis contra Anglorum nationem vicecomitem de Lanark interfecit, et cum eo multos alios.
  • Qui: = "he"; in English, we prefer not to begin a sentence with a relative pronoun referring to a previous sentence.
  • cum: subord. conj. = "since"
  • esset: imprefect subjunctive → progressive past
  • detestans: present participle → progressive active
  • de: prep. + ABL(X) = "from X"
  • Lanark: Proper name of an area in South Central Scotland; ABL
  • cum: prep. + ABL(X) = "with X"
22
Ex eo igitur tempore congregati sunt ad eum omnes qui erant corde amaro, et quorum animae erant in angustia, et qui opprimebantur sub servitute et gravi iugo Anglicorum.
  • congregati sunt: the passive is used with nearly the same meaning as congregaverunt se.
  • corde: ABL(X) = "with X"
  • amaro: = "bitter"
  • angustia: = "anguish"
23
Factus est ergo princeps eorum et dux.
  • Factus est: verb frame → SBJ was made/became X, where X is same CASE as SBJ (same NUMBER & GENDER where possible)
24
Ipse enim Anglos undique prosternens, et contra eos in omnibus proficiens, in brevi tempore omnes magnates Scotiae, vi et virtute probitatis suae, velint nolint, suo subiecit imperio; et, sic pacatis omnibus, viriliter se manutenuit, et ad expugnanda castra fortiora, et loca fortia ubi Anglici principabantur sub dicione et dominio Scotorum reducere, omnibus viribus studuit.
  • prosternens: present participle → progressinve [time] active, where time/tense is indicated by context
  • proficiens: = "be successful; present participle → progressinve [time] active, where time/tense is indicated by context
  • velint nolint: = "willy nilly"
  • manutenuit: = "maintain/keep"; verb frame → SBJ maintained X in manner/state(Y), where X is ACC and Y is ADV
